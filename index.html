GOOD TO KNOW

A Few Things to Know

Everything you need to know before we begin telling your story.

How do we book your services?

Simply reach out through WhatsApp with your event date, location, and preferred coverage. Our team will guide you through the process.

Do you offer photography and videography?

Yes. We offer photography, videography, and combined coverage for your celebration.

Do you travel for weddings?

Absolutely. We are available for weddings beyond our usual coverage area. Travel arrangements can be discussed before booking.

When can we expect to receive our wedding photos?

Good news to all clients that book with Symbiotic! You will get 50 to 100 edited photos which are personally selected by us as early as 7 working days after your wedding! For all photos taken during the shoot, you will get them, guaranteed in 14 working days.

Can we customize our wedding package?

Every celebration is different. Share your plans with us, and we'll explore the coverage options that best suit your event.

How can we check your availability?

Send us your event date through WhatsApp, and we'll be happy to check our team's availability.
